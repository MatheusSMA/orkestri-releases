# Orkestri privacy policy

Orkestri is a desktop app that runs on your own computer.

## Google Calendar

When you choose "Sign in with Google" on a Calendar card, Orkestri asks Google for access to your calendar events
(`calendar.events`). It uses that access only to:

- show your events on the Calendar card;
- create in your Google Calendar the events you (or an agent you connected to the card) add on that card.

The sign-in token is stored only on your computer, encrypted for your Windows user. Your events are kept only in your
Orkestri workspace on your computer. Nothing is sent to any server of Orkestri or to anyone else: Orkestri talks only
to Google's own servers.

You can remove the access at any time with "Disconnect Google" on the card, or at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
